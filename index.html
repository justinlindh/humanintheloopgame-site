Twenty years, five eras

Start in a garage in 2019, before anyone has heard of a large language model. Then the chatbots arrive, then the agents, then everyone buys everyone.

People, not headcount

Everyone has a desk, a mood and opinions in Yak, the company chat. Automate the work they were proud of and they coast, burn out, then post a farewell in #general.

Ship it, then read the reviews

Pick a category and an angle, build, launch, and let TechCrunchy decide how you feel about it. Some combos are brilliant. Some are AI-powered spreadsheet number 47.

Call the outage a stress test

Rogue agents, breaches and outages at 3am. Big decisions show up in the office as props: the letter on the desk, the rival's sign across the street, the smoke from the rack.
