Forty-three seconds of company history

Survive the AI eras. First chatbots. Then agents. Then whatever the agents hire.

What you do all day

People, not headcount

Everyone has a desk, a mood and a breaking point. Automate the work they were proud of and they coast, burn out, then leave.

Talk back in Yak

Answer your team's threads, or post a pep talk, a meme or "who broke prod?" yourself. They react, they reply, and they argue back. A meme during an outage gets the reaction it deserves.

Ship it, then read the reviews

Pick a category and an angle, build, launch, and let TechCrunchy decide how you feel about it. Some combos are brilliant. Some are AI-powered spreadsheet number 47.

Call the outage a stress test

Rogue agents, breaches and outages at 3am. Red lights, people running, and a call to make before the customers notice.

Decisions you can see

Big calls show up in the office: a novelty cheque the size of a door, a whiteboard that says the market has spoken, the founders hiding while a stranger tries the product.

Automate it, and live with it

Agents are cheaper than people, right up until the cloud bill develops feelings. Hand the work to the machines and watch the rack run hot, the pull requests pile up overnight, and the people who used to do it find something else to care about.

The Waffle Party is real

Reward your team with waffles. And a mandatory dance break. Nobody asked for the dance break.

From the office

One career, five eras

2019

Classic SaaS

Web apps, mobile apps, APIs, and a lot of pricing pages. Nobody has heard of a large language model.

about 2022

The ChatGBT Moment

A chatbot writes a sonnet about your churn rate and the whole industry loses its mind.

about 2025

Agents

The software now does the job instead of helping with it. Someone has to watch it do the job.

about 2029

Consolidation

Everyone is buying everyone. The vendors change their prices, their models, and their minds.

about 2034

The Plateau

Everyone has the same models now. What is left is people, taste, and whether anyone trusts you.
